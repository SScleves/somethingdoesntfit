## 2. The rule I placed by

Three kinds of placement, in the proportion the guardrails ask for:

1. **Thread** (real, documented, survives Frank): on the page, in dialogue where possible. Examples: the 1953 panel text, COMETA's four phases, MKUltra's receipts, Crypto AG, Room 641A, the Beria pages, Enoch 104.
2. **Noise, earned by being false**: on the page only to be killed or to stand beside the real thing. Phantom time, the full-moon video, the Mandela effect, Tartaria, the Tesla quote, the city under Khafre.
3. **Mechanic / under the floor**: never said, but the chapter knows it. Penemue, Yima, entropy as lost light, the four hollows. These are for you and me, not the reader.

Reality rule kept: no fictional edit on a real book; real figures only doing documented things; the invented things (the List entries, the thin book, the seat roster) develop the plot and never justify it.

## 3. Interludes: the set, settled

[Certain] v8.2 lists six non-James historical pieces: Down House 1860, Croton 530 BC, Mexico City 1994, Washington 1953, Urfa 1994, Leiden 1931. The older manuscript had four others, cut in v6: Gondar 1770, Nuremberg 1561, Rockefeller Museum 1978/1991, and the Bratislava cutaway.

Same man, two deaths, and the second is labelled a real interlude about an invented person, which breaks the "real events only" amendment. **Recommendation: cut Mexico City 1994.** The canal road in Utrecht is stronger and already echoes Zwolle.
2. [Probable] **Croton 530 BC is a paragraph, not an interlude.** Pythagoras and the dog is one anecdote (Xenophanes, via Diogenes Laertius). It can't carry 800 words of real material without padding. **Recommendation: fold it into Reading with a pen** as something Beard Man says, or keep it as a 150-word epigraph-piece before the epilogue, where it lands hardest. Don't give it a chapter slot.
3. [Probable] **Gondar 1770 should come back.** It is the best-researched real piece you have (Bruce, the Bodleian shelf, fifty years unread, Laurence 1821), and the library now has six Enoch entries that make it load-bearing: Penemue, the shepherds' scribe, 104:10, the corrupted calendar, the four hollows. 4. [Assumption] **Maní 1562** is a candidate only if you want a fourth real piece about books; it would sit in Part Three before The sheets (burning the books vs. giving them back). I would not add it in this round. Four real interludes is enough.
