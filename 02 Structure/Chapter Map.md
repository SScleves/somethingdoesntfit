**Core decision recorded here:** the thin untitled book = **the professor's Record** — a list of edits (book / page / printing / before → after), ~40 entries, no thesis. Tresidder = entry 11. ## ACT 1 — THE WIRE (ch. 1–9)

1. ✅ **The Funeral.** The 85% yes. The flash. Buddy at the bottom step. The corridor planted. No vow; the search begins (the only "beginning" in the book). HEART.
2. ✅ **The Good Life.** Dogs, grief visits, work (Frank, "based on what?"), the hunt, the Record, golf, the tabs. "I'd have signed for another forty years of it." HEART.
3. ✅ **Reading with a Pen.** The folder named Reading; incident reports on death; the two shapes (the rhyme, the denial); Ana's medal; the deleted post; Buddy skips the front nine; the professor's crates; the syllabus shelf; the thin book. INNER seed.
4. ✅ **The Sentence.** *Transmitted to the early* → *arisen among the first.* Same plates, untouched typos, the blemish on 118. Scully. Tresidder vanished 1929; second impression 1931. **doesn't fit.** — **Rung 1.**
5. **Am I the Error?** He builds the case against himself (three theories, the author one already dead); the record-wound surfaces as confession; the submarine scene; the shrink committee. "Grief could explain why I cared about the word. It could not put the word on the page." *(redraft pending against ch. 4 v8 + Competence Ledger)*
6. **The Publisher Trail / The Window.** Who reopened the plates in 1931 — a small house, absorbed twice, records "lost in the war" (the first lab coat he can't get to fit). INNER opens: first psychedelic experience, clocked as a window, not a door. Ana friction sharpens. He finally opens the thin book. It's a list.
7. **The List.** Structure of the professor's Record; forty entries; Tresidder is #11. He verifies the cheapest entry (two paperbacks, one afternoon) — it holds. It's not one book. One entry names a hill outside Urfa and a word: *redefinition.* — **Rung 2 enters.**
8. **The Goalpost.** Göbekli Tepe understood: the definition of "hunter-gatherer" revised to protect the timeline — his wound at civilizational scale. Buried deliberately: a cover-up with a date on it. Hobbyist → investigator. He decides to work the list. **Break into Two.**
9. **Forty Years.** The last warm chapter: golf with all four, Ana laughing at the sixth, Buddy in the cart. He hides the list under the Record's password. Frank asks him something at work he answers too well. Act 1 ends on the life he's about to spend. HEART.

## ACT 2A — THE PATTERN (ch. 10–17)

10. **Where Are You.** Ana anchor: the jokes thinning; the knitting still paused; she asks what he's *for* lately. B-story locked. HEART.
11. **The Bringers.** Weir-mode: entries in the mythology books — the knowledge-bringer punished in every pantheon, old gods rewritten as demons. He sees the *direction* of every edit for the first time: always toward *nothing was given.* — **Rung 3.**
12. **Withdrawn for Conservation.** Treasure-hunt set-piece: entry 17, the 1963 Anatolian survey, a national library, a polite refusal, the year 1994. The list is alive; someone is working it from the other side. CLOCK whisper.
13. **Beard Man.** He asks about the professor. How he died. The house. The family who kept the furniture. A daughter who says "he'd stopped sleeping." — **Rung 4 begins.**
14. **The Furniture.** The professor's death: quiet, fast, plausible. A scrubbed obituary. A dealer who asks one question too many. First indirect contact with the opposition — they never show a face. He starts hiding the search from Ana "to protect her."
15. **The Grammar.** Three more entries verified; the tool-mark named: givers deleted, dates pulled down, *early* → *first.* Not an idea — a *habit*, across a century. INNER: psychedelics plateau; he starts meditating, clumsily. The faces begin. Hands. "Not the first time" starts unlocking.
16. **The Last Entry.** Unfinished. A place, not a page. Coordinates in the professor's hand, and a date he'd circled — three weeks after he died. Where he was going.
17. **The Rope Has Two Ends.** Midpoint. The foundations behind the withdrawn survey are the foundations behind a bunker consortium. History is a forecast. He stops being harmless. Radar pings for real. — **Rung 5. CLOCK starts.**

## ACT 2B — THE CLOCK (ch. 18–25)

18. **Enjoy It While It Lasts.** Frank's tools arrive at his own company; oversight quietly gutted; a stranger on the fairway who never asks about him (the deferred golf beat — first Keeper contact, reader-aware, no face). Surveillance noticed the way he notices things: a small wrongness nobody bothered to hide from people who don't look. ⚠ *AI fork made concrete.*
19. **The Chart.** He builds the golden-age pattern: civilizations fall when about to succeed. Suffering is the leash; comfort is the crack. Predict-vs-cause opens. — **Rung 6.**
20. **The Shape of Them.** What the Keepers are, institutionally — through the paper trail only, never a monologue. Two tiers implied. ⚠ *Keepers-institutionally decided here.*
21. **The Gorilla Mask.** The P-59 story surfaces; then it happens to him — his deleted forum post from ch. 3 exists, under his name, with additions he never wrote. Discredited in advance. He can't persuade anyone; he never could.
22. **On Land.** All Is Lost I. Ana's ultimatum, or she leaves for her mother's. The jokes stop — the evening promised in ch. 2. HEART at full price.
23. **The Vet.** All Is Lost II. Buddy's bad night that ends okay; the clock audible. The professor's trail ends in a broken person (the daughter, or a collaborator who got the visit). Whiff of death everywhere, Buddy lives.
24. **The Crossing I.** Rock bottom; the sober deep meditation. The wall gives. The lives — brutal, short, love cut off mid-sentence. The flashes were never metaphor. Hands. *(Three Keys rule: sober.)*
25. **The Crossing II.** The corridor opens — the father, grief with a container at last, twenty years late. The shrink history detonates (they certified him fine and never found this). The separation engine named. The list re-reads itself: the physics entries, the psychiatry entries — same list. INNER + LADDER = one seam. **The title is the reveal.**

## ACT 3 — THE OFFER (ch. 26–33)

26. **Same Weapon, Opposite Intent.** Exposure is pointless (the mask works). Crack the hoard. He tells Ana everything — she becomes the reason, not the casualty. "This was always about seeing you again." **Break into Three.**
27. **The Place.** The last entry resolved: the hoard located. ⚠ *Hoard decided here (Cappadocia-style / Cuba / Axum-style).*
28. **Reading the Door.** The approach — by records, not by breaking (Competence Ledger). He finds the way in the way he found the sentence: the thing everyone checks the lazy way.
29. **Inside.** The two tiers face to face; those who know, those who inherited. The reset schedule as a *document* a monitoring man can read; the AI steering snaps into focus. ⚠ *Reset mechanism + AI steering concrete here.* — **Rung 7.**
30. **The Offer.** Not a bomb. He splits them; some refuse. The hoard opens to the world in-story; the reader never gets the "how."
31. **The Door.** The cost: he holds it and doesn't walk through. ⚠ *Dies vs. stays decided here.*
32. **Coda I — Buddy.** Flash-forward: an old man, an ancient dog. When Buddy goes, no borrowed phrases. Grief with a container. Love individuates; the collective is the default. The funeral, inverted.
33. **Coda II — The Wake.** Some-when later. Partial memory, happy to pursue. Machines doing the ugly work, people unhurried. A young dog doing one thing only Buddy did. An almost-recognition — possible, not promised. The thesis line, once.
