## The mechanism every James chapter must show
James holds two versions of one thing that should match and don't. He does one thing from his trade to check the difference (compare, capture, build, ask, test, sign, wait, tell, refuse). The check needs a person, who chooses. The check is seen and counted. The organization answers by agreeing and reissuing a duller version, and that reissue is his next clue. He leaves each chapter with an object he did not have, and somebody pays. Join chapters with "therefore" or "but", never "and then".

## Rules that do not bend
James can't hack, break in, impersonate, threaten or pressure anyone; a door opens only when a person chooses. Reveals arrive as objects in his hands. The organization discredits and buys; killing is rare and somebody's mistake. Real anomalies may be real; edits, people and companies are invented; never an invented edit on a real book. James is not a chosen one. The book stays funny. Every table James sits at has a dog under it, and the organization's tables never do. American spelling. Numbers in prose as words.
