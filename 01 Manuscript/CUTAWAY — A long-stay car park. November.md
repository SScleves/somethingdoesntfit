# A long-stay car park
### November

He did the accreditation first because it had a renewal date on it and dates are the only things that generate attention on their own.

It took ten minutes on a laptop in a car. The professional body's portal accepted a resignation on grounds of retirement, thanked him for thirty-one years of membership, and offered to send a certificate to an address he did not provide. He then wrote to the two clients who had the name on a contract, from the same account, in four lines, saying that ill health had made it necessary to hand over and that his colleague would be in touch.

He did not delete anything. Deleting generates a gap and a gap is a shape.

Julian Kalder went on existing. He simply stopped being current, in the ordinary way that a man of sixty-four stops being current, and in about four years nobody would be able to say when it had happened.

At the end he sat with the cursor over the last field for a moment, then typed the sister's name into a search box, looked at the result without opening it, and closed the window.

---

The car park was long-stay and about a third full, and it had a view of a fence and, past the fence, of aircraft.

The envelope had come the day before, at the desk of a hotel, addressed to a name he had used twice.

Inside: a passport that was real, a card, a folded printout of a lease on a flat in a city he had not been to since 2019, and one sheet.

He read the sheet twice.

It gave him a name. It gave him a trade — something to do with valuations, adjacent to the last one, near enough that the manner would carry. It gave a date for the flat and a date for a meeting.

Then, at the bottom, under a rule, three lines that were not about him:

> *Note for planning. Review point brought forward to March. §4 set will be reissued at the review; assume current bands are provisional. Assets in transit should be settled before the reissue rather than after.*

He read that part four times.

March was eleven months earlier than March had been in September.

He put the sheet on the passenger seat, took a photograph of nothing out of the windscreen, and sat for a while watching an aircraft come in over the fence.

Then he took out the other phone and rang the number he was allowed to ring twice a year.

---

"It's early," Ruys said.

"You've moved the review."

"I haven't moved anything. I am told things in the same envelope you are, four hours sooner." A pause that was not defensive. "Is that a complaint?"

"It's a question. Am I to finish the file or settle it."

"Settle it," said Ruys. "That is what *before the reissue* means and you know perfectly well what it means, which is why you rang me instead of reading it again."

Somewhere on the line there was paper moving.

"For what it's worth," Ruys said, "and I have no expectation of your caring, I did write in. It's a poor decision. The bands have been drifting for two years and the correct response to drift is to widen the band, which is what we have always done, and instead somebody has decided to bring the whole thing forward and re-cut it. It'll be justified in a review note in eighteen months and the note will be perfectly sensible and by then nobody will remember what it was like before."

"Is that a problem for me."

"No," said Ruys. "You'll have a flat."

---

"The Anatolia matter," he said.

"Is not yours. That is now an internal question and it is being handled internally and I would advise you against being interested in it."

"Nine hundred people."

"Nine hundred people have been given a sum to do," Ruys said, "by a man with a spreadsheet, and about four hundred of them will do it, and about eighty will not like the answer, and of those eighty perhaps a dozen will do something, and what a dozen people do is contract law." His voice did not change at all. "It is expensive and it is tedious and it is not, in the end, difficult. There has never been a version of this that did not involve paying people, and there has never been a version where the number of people who had to be paid did not go up."

"And Klippe."

"Nothing. Leave him entirely alone, permanently, and that is not a preference, it is now written down." A dry sound. "He has arranged himself so that he is worth more to us alive and quiet than in any other condition, which is the first genuinely competent thing anybody outside this building has done in my career, and I have some respect for it and I would be grateful if you did not go and play golf."

---

He hung up and sat in the car for a while with the sheet on the passenger seat.

There is nothing else to report about him. He did not think about the conservator in Leiden, or about a corridor in 2017, or about a woman on a step who talked about a blanket, or about a dog that would not come near him. He did not reflect on the review point or on what a reissued §4 would mean or on whether any of it was warranted, and there is no reason to believe he ever had.

He got out, took a bag from the boot, locked the car, and left it in a long-stay bay in November with three weeks paid on it.

Then he walked to the terminal under a name that has not appeared anywhere in this account, and that nobody in it will ever hear, and that is not, in all probability, the one he was given as a child.
