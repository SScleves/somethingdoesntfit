# Beard Man
Second-hand book dealer at [[The Market]]. No names exchanged — "for all I know he's a Kevin." Talks like a leaking pipe; 90% nonsense; interesting-wrong. Mid-sentence since before the narrator was born (a comet that ruined a prophet).
- Source of the professor's crates; threw in the twelfth book "because twelve sounds better" (ambiguous: whimsy or completion — the narrator resents every minute spent on it).
- ch. 13: asked about the professor — how he died, the family, the house.
- The one audience that out-nerds him: he's the straight man for once.
- Coda: the crate ending — someone else's estate, a stranger kneels, the thin book at the bottom, "twelve sounds better," cut.
