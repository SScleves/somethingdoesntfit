# Mora
Black Doberman, six. All ears and no brakes. Greets like a resurrection; always hungry; sleeps upside down like a crashed helicopter. Duck poop = caviar; she's gross.
- He wanted Blade; Ana said that was fucked up; he still doesn't know why.
- Two states at home: with you, or waiting for you (his misfired did-you-know, ch. 2).
- Indoors at 4 a.m. she plays fetch until someone dies. The toy: a rope once, now a damp grey ecosystem that smells like the Paris Métro in July. It must *leave his hand* — the grammar's second half (*transmitted*).
