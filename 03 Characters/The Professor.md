# The Professor
Dead before ch. 3. His books came to [[Beard Man]]'s crates; the family kept the furniture. Annotated like he was arguing with authors (*no* — just *no*).
- Grandson of Tresidder's student. Inherited the obsession and [[The List]] (two hands: the older ink his grandfather's, the rest his; the last entry's "Ask" shakier, added later).
- The shelf was his conclusions: Ubik, A Canticle for Leibowitz, Blindsight (still buying at the end), Borges misfiled (Tlön — deliberate). Payoff ch. 11–15: "I wasn't looking at a collection. I was looking at a man's conclusions."
- Death: quiet, fast, plausible (ch. 14). A daughter who says "he'd stopped sleeping." Method used on him: unknown until then.
- He is the essay author — died before writing the argument. Mentor as corpse ([[Hero's Journey Mapping]]).
