# Ana
Wife. 38. Raised half by [[The Grandmother]]. Background unspecified. The B story and the ending's detonator.
- Squeezes his hand when nervous — the *not squeezing* at the funeral is how we meet her grief.
- Grief pattern: fine at dinner, destroyed by a drawer. The unfinished pale-green knitting, paused mid-row, on the shelf in [[The House]].
- Reads upside down better than most read right side up. Watches him do the math. "You've got the face."
- Names: Mulder / Scully; the Record = "strange sci-fi poetry" ("it isn't sci-fi and I hate poetry — she knows both things"); tinfoil hat size every December (love, never the mask).
- Her argument is never *is it true* — it's *where are you* / "I'd like my husband on land for a while" (ch. 5 submarine scene).
- The forward-flash (ch. 2): "one evening, much later, when the jokes stopped" → ch. 22.
- [[The Faucet]] arc through her: charmed → "you've started telling me the real ones" → the silence after ch. 20 ("you haven't told me a did-you-know in three weeks").
- ch. 26: he tells her the *shape of the war* — "I can't beat a hundred. I can compromise one." Not the switch.
- Coda: the almost-recognition — possible, not promised. The hand in the funeral flash was hers (never confirmed on-page before the coda).
