# Buddy
Old dog. Twelve, grey to the eyebrows, slowing. Watches from the bottom step. Two thumps of the tail — "the short version of a dance he used to do."
- **Canon: Buddy survives the book.** Death only in the coda flash-forward, met with expectation.
- The clock at house scale: skips the front nine (ch. 3); supervises from the cart; the vet night (ch. 23) ends okay.
- Licensed listener: gets the second-impression lecture (ch. 4); *arises* on a mumbled word and a lazy hand from the floor — the grammar's first half.
- "You'd tell me if you knew something, right?" — two thumps, a sideways look, one small bark.
- Coda Movement 1: buried with no borrowed phrases. Love individuated him ([[Group Souls and Individuation]]). The coda's young dog does one thing only Buddy did (TBD).
