# Engines — how other novels make conspiracy material move a story

A MindBook-style reading library for *Something Doesn't Fit*. Each folder is one novel, read in full, for one question: **how does the conspiracy force the hero to do the next thing**, and what would James *do* differently if we borrowed it.

## Start here (for an agent helping Santi)

1. Read the problem in `PIPELINE.md` ("What Step 0 found"): in Part Two James mostly receives documents; nothing stops him and nothing he does can go wrong.
2. Read `08 Research/Comps/ADDENDUM 05 — Conspiracy engines (what moves a conspiracy plot).md` once it exists: the verdict on the four candidate fixes and the Part Two chapter table.
3. For a specific book, open its `00-map.md`. The engine table (section 2) and the transfer proposals (section 5) are the point.
4. To check a claim, follow the map's citation to the section note (`NN-*.md`), which says what happens in those chapters and quotes the line.
5. For a quick answer, `91-qa.md` holds the questions an SDF agent is likely to ask, answered with pointers.

## The four candidate fixes the books are tested against

- **A. The gate** (*11/22/63*): the hero may not act until he rules out the bigger explanation.
- **B. The canary** (*Foucault's Pendulum*, reversed): something invented is believed, and the believers reveal themselves.
- **C. The front** (*Illuminatus!*): each solved layer is the cover story for the next.
- **D. Lock and key** (*The Last Templar*, *The Eight*): each door needs one specific key; each helper one specific truth.

All proposals here are proposals. Santi's decisions are marked PLACEHOLDER. Book texts are never stored in this repo.
