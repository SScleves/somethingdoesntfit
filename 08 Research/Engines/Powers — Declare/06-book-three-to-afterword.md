## Why it grips
- **The record runs the clock.** Chapter 17 opens in Philby's head and walks through documented Beirut, January 1963: Elliott's confrontation, the typed partial confession, Lunn taking over, the dinner at the Balfour-Pauls'. Powers hangs the invented departure on that real night. Philby phones home, his son Harry answers, and Mammalian presses down the plunger. The next day would have been the wedding anniversary. The real calendar makes the invented scene hurt.
- **A lore lecture that ends in a bombshell.** Mammalian's tent briefing (Hippolytus, Bishop Jacob, drogue stones) looks like exposition until "The brothers are divided" is answered: the two sons are Philby and Hale. The scene turns from information to injury.
- **Cross-cut.** Elena's section ends with the rockets armed ("Montrez-moi"). Chapter 18 pays it off from the ground, then from her cabin: she knows his posture and veers.
- **The title is a bet.** The 1948 hand of seven-card stud, "high-low declare", is finished in a Moscow park. The cards hardly matter. All that counts is which way each man declares: high wins Elena, low wins immortality. Philby goes low and Hale goes high. Philby's prize is a fraud: the root will die on the shot in his back.
- **Still funny.** Philby: "t-two middle-aged Englishmen k-killed ten spit-spit—fucking—Spetsnaz". Theodora's 1918 plan to parade Lenin in his underwear. "The Beetles".

## How the conspiracy moves the story here
The hidden order (Theodora's SOE remnant, Declare) is what stops Hale from just going home. Once the djinn are dead, Hale knows that a Prime Minister authorised a "sorcerous" strike on the USSR, so in Theodora's logic he has to be killed: "we’ve killed bigger heroes, famous ones". Nobody tries to stop him knowing. Instead he is scheduled for a quiet death and an "enduring disgrace" of his last cover.

Hale beats this with a document. He has written the story down as a book, *Declare*, and left it with a Belgian solicitor, who sends it to the press if Hale's yearly Christmas card (with a photo dated by a newspaper) fails to arrive. Then he offers the conspiracy what it needs, the only man who can kill Philby, inside a cover story Whitehall will accept (a journalist investigating Burobin). The secret goes from being the thing that will kill him to his ticket into Moscow.

The second hidden order, Machikha Nash, gets a community: the Gray People, defectors who learn whom they serve only "when we’ve surrendered our passports".

## How the lore gets in
- **Mammalian's briefing (ch. 17), about 1,300 words, conversation.** Legend is mixed with climbing drill ("recite the multiplication tables") and it ends in an order ("Up"). The brothers reveal lands mid-lecture, so it does not stall.
- **Elena's catch-up (ch. 17), about 1,500 words, backstory.** It is told in the past perfect from inside a helicopter heading for the target. The countdown in the present-tense frame ("Une dizaine minutes") keeps it moving.
- **Moscow travelogue, about 1,500 words.** This is the one slow stretch. Powers keeps it going with a deadline (noon on the 22nd) and two errands Hale keeps putting off.
- **Afterword and Author's Note, about 1,900 words plus 19 footnotes.** The Afterword gives documented facts: Philby died in 1988 and was *buried*, and the USSR fell in 1991. That closes the chain the Epilogue set up, where cremation would melt the shot and burial lets the ghula take it in. A public fact becomes the last plot beat. The Note then lists the real "wobbles" with sources: the fox mourned like a father, the coat lined with red fox fur, the "lost" Lawrence files, the Ararat photograph, feet bruised from a walk, *The Birds*.

## Mechanism notes
- **The method.** Powers treats documented facts as fixed points and invents only their cause, like astronomers who infer a planet from wobbles in other orbits. He quotes real books (*Seven Pillars*, *The Empty Quarter*, *My Silent War*) accurately and reads them as cover stories, never inventing a passage. That is the SDF rule: the real anomaly stays real, and the explanation is the invention.
- **Ending on a checkable fact.** The plot's last event, the burial, is something the reader can check. *[Inference]* For SDF, the final proof could be an odd real fact anyone can look up, which only the invented organisation explains.
- **A (gate) as leverage.** Hale may not come home until he holds something that makes killing him cost more than letting him live. He gets past the gate with an object, not force.
- **B (canary), reversed.** Hale sells Philby a value he knows is false, and Philby's belief settles the bet.
- **C (front).** The layers are stacked: the Burobin journalism covers the plan to kill Philby, and that plan covers Hale's real errand (Elena). Theodora builds his own deniability into it: "I’ll be as shocked as anyone else".
- **D (lock and key).** Doors open on private phrases. The Arabian Nights line about the fish and the old covenant [from memory, not from the text: its source] opens the Gray Person. "Segne mich" (the GRU code for *trust me*) and "I did not kill Cassagnac" open Elena. She had already decided on the helicopter, so the key only confirms her own choice.
- **Fifth: the hero writes the leak.** Hale's insurance is the novel itself. For SDF, this could be James's manuscript held in escrow, which the organisation would rather discredit than suppress.

## Quotes
- "I could not change or disregard any of the recorded facts, nor rearrange any days of the calendar" (Author's Note): the whole method.
- "then I tried to figure out what momentous but unrecorded fact could explain them all." (Author's Note): invent the cause, never the fact.
- "Into his silence I’ve presumed to fit the admittedly extravagant—but, I think, consistent—premise of this story." (Author's Note): the invention goes in the gaps of the record.
- "Whatever the outcome, I declared high." (Epilogue): the title paid off as a moral choice.

## Flags
- Real people are made complicit: Philby betrays SAS men and serves the djinn, and Burgess relays MiG photos. A legend in chapter 17 says Declare "dealt with" Turing, T. E. Lawrence and Kitchener. The Note hints at the black car at Lawrence's crash. SDF must not do this.
- No invented edits on real books. Powers quotes real texts accurately and only reinterprets them, which is the model SDF should copy.
