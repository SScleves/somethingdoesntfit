## Movements
There is no protagonist line here. The appendices are documents outside the story, and the plot is over. Two small scenes inside them move a character.

## Why it grips
- **The book keeps going after it ends.** The appendices give minor characters futures (Aleph: Saul and Barney's agency; Nun: Pricefixer shot two years later, Markoff Chaney in 1984). Back matter carries plot, so every appendix must be read.
- **Every explanation is a trap.** Kaph explains the "magic" roses as a gypsy switch, then leaves the three thieves dead in a ditch. Mem explains Mama Sutra's reading as scraps of *Manhattan Melodrama*, then notes it was the last film the man shot outside the Biograph saw. Each debunking hands the reader a new connection.
- **The narrators argue with themselves.** Footnotes ask "Do you believe that?" (Gimmel, Yod), and a character reads the manuscript and says the authors made the evidence up (Tzaddi). The reader never settles which layer is the joke.
- **Withholding as a gag.** Cheth is one sentence long. Mem's last question, "Where are the missing eight appendices?", is answered "Censored."

## How the conspiracy moves the story here
There is no story left, so the conspiracy moves the *reader*. Lamed says the book is part of Operation Mindfuck and has programmed the reader, then tests it: "you have lost track of Joe Malik's mysterious dogs by now." A thread planted and dropped on purpose, checked at the end: a canary set in the reader. Yod makes OM a method: nobody knows who is in it, so an outsider must treat everything as part of it (paranoia) or nothing. Mem item 8 shows how a real connection survives: a historian's book records both the vanished eagle (p. 123) and the dead censor (p. 369), and "Neither he nor anyone else ever connected the two occurrences." The truth sits in the record, findable and never found.

## How the lore gets in
- **Pseudo-scholarship with real citations (Aleph, Daleth, Vau, Zain), about 6,000 words.** Real page numbers carry invented claims in. Each ends by cutting to a character (Simon's toilet paper, Rebecca's books), so no stretch goes long without a person.
- **The history-of-history (Gimmel), about 5,500 words, a mock treatise.** The longest stall. It survives on the story at its centre (Weishaupt, drugged on a misread letter, finds a theory of history in a misprinted *Necronomicon*) and ends on a scene, George Dorn silencing his mother.
- **Hagbard's booklet (Teth), about 3,500 words.** Pure argument; it stalls, and the authors joke in advance that Hagbard wanted all of it printed.
- **Operation Mindfuck (Yod), about 2,000 words, a field manual.** The mock letter of appointment is reproduced whole: lore as an object a target would hold.
- **Q&A and dramatis personae (Mem, Nun), about 3,500 words.** One-paragraph entries, each a joke or a fact. The fastest lore in the book.

Could SDF's List or interludes do this? The List already has the form (documents, no commentary). Illuminatus adds: back matter that carries plot (an entry that quietly finishes a minor character); explaining then re-mystifying (a mundane "after" with a marginal note that isn't); and a test of the reader, planted early and checked late. Proposal, PLACEHOLDER: one List entry whose margin note points back to a detail the reader was allowed to forget.

## Mechanism notes
- **C, the front, laid out in full (Gimmel).** Weishaupt keeps the Law of Fives secret and pushes Joachim's three ages through a "front man" (Hegel). Public theory (three stages) covers private theory (five), which comes from a drugged misreading of a misprint, which the authors doubt, which the A:.A:. call projection. The bottom layer is a joke. That is mechanism C's danger: if every layer is a front, the reader stops caring which is true. SDF needs a floor (what happens when we die) where the fronts stop.
- **B, the canary as method (Yod).** Project Jake (one official gets absurd letters from dozens of real-sounding cabals), the honorary memberships, and Citizens Against Drug Abuse, which campaigns to ban catnip so the drug war looks absurd. SDF's thermostat with the owners swapped: rebels fund nonsense to discredit the state; SDF's Keepers fund it to discredit the question. It works when the target cannot tell "a conspiracy composed entirely of lunatics" from a public "much more imaginative and less stodgy" than he assumed (Yod). Nun gives the cleanest trick for a leak: pamphlets stamped both TOP SECRET and FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE, and no one can work out who stamped which.
- **A, the gate, by way of the investigator (Beth).** "The harder I looked, the more things there were that fit." James's risk exactly: a gate that makes him rule out his own pattern-seeing would cost him real confidence in his nose.
- **D, lock and key (Lamed).** Hagbard opens Miss Portinari with one private truth, her hidden symbol, and leaves her to come to him; she does, two weeks later. A person chooses.
- **Layered membership (Daleth).** The Assassins' lower grades know only that a hidden meaning exists and that they must obey, like SDF's helpers with one task and the word "continuity". The order fell to outsiders it had never infiltrated: a weakness SDF could use (someone the count never reached).
- **Fifth: the misprint as engine (Gimmel).** An error in one printing produces a whole doctrine: SDF's premise inverted, a corrupted edition *creating* belief. The book is fictional, so it is clean to borrow.

## Flags
Real people and organisations named are everywhere. Strictly:
- **Named as Illuminati or complicit:** Meyer Amschel Rothschild, de Sade and Sir Francis Dashwood as "the original Five" with Weishaupt and Kolmer (Gimmel; the Rothschild line repeats an antisemitic trope). Hegel as the Illuminati's "front man" (Gimmel). Joyce warned and blinded by the Illuminati (Gimmel). George Washington (the "Weishaupt impersonation theory", Aleph). The authors suggest "Adolf Hitler's link with the Illuminati was not invented" (Lamed footnote).
- **Real assassination figures:** Kerry Thornley, named as a friend of Oswald's whom Garrison accused (Nun). The Warren Report called a "novel" (Nun); Capone and the FBI at the Biograph (Mem).
- **Other real people and organisations:** Eldridge Cleaver (invented dialogue), Henry Ford and the *Protocols*, Nixon, Mayor Daley, Leary (Nun, Lamed); astrological lists of real people (Gimmel); ACLU employing the fictional detectives (Aleph), John Birch Society, AMA as a band name, Universal Life Church.
- **Real text reinterpreted:** Crowley's *Book of Lies*, chapter 23, quoted in full as a working spell (Tzaddi). Nothing is edited, but a real book is given an invented secret use. SDF must not do this to a real book.
- **Misprint on a book:** the scrambled *Necronomicon* passage (Gimmel) is a fictional book, so it is allowed.
