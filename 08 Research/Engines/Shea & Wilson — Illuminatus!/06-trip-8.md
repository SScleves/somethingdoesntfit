## Why it grips
- **The same facts told twice, with the values flipped.** Stella tells Hagbard's Mohawk story (he refused the dam job, helped the evicted families move), and the narrator corrects her at once: he was their lawyer, too ashamed to say so. Thousands of words later Mama Sutra tells it back: a "much-needed dam", "unbalanced behavior in the courtroom", a Mafia-paid Harvard degree. The reader can compare both versions, so the lie entertains instead of confusing. This is the strongest scene for C.
- **One scene played twice, from different heads.** Harry Coin's gun trial is first Harry's terror, then Hagbard's side: "variation #2 of the third basic tactic", with Hagbard half hoping Harry will shoot so he can dodge the Peos decision. Repeated lines ("Three days and three minutes are both too long", "Ready for destruction of enemy ships") mark where in the old scene the new layer sits.
- **A withheld name.** Dillinger at Dealey Plaza sees the real killer's face ("Christ!" John said. "Him?") and the scene cuts away.
- **Orientation.** The jumps are always stamped: "half a mile below and twelve hours earlier", "in present time", the present frame in parentheses while a flashback runs, one run-on sentence that pulls Chips, the Midget, Dillinger and Weishaupt through the same space-time "tug". Each voice has a fixed idiom (Chips's "Not High Church at all", Harry's "Ace", Knight's agency jargon), so you know who is talking before you get a name. Refrains ("Now look what you made me do", "No remission without blood") stitch the strands together.

## How the conspiracy moves the story here
The weapon here is the **counter-story**, not force. Mama Sutra does not hide evidence from Danny; she gets ahead of it. Before he describes the memos she asks whether they "portrayed them in an unfavorable light?", having already said that every leak against the Illuminati is enemy propaganda. The reader has just seen Hagbard refuse to kill, so the inversion is visible to us and hidden from Danny. This is SDF's "discredit, don't prevent" working on the page: his findings are left alone and given a frame in which they point the other way.

The break is small and cheap: a clearance detail. Hassan i Sabbah X, handing Knight a password, adds that Mama isn't cleared, and Miss Mao turns out to spy on her for three masters. The grand account Danny took for the whole answer becomes one ring of five. Knight's memoir repeats the move: each recruitment story holds until the next superior laughs at it. Winifred's speech supplies the method SDF's helpers need: each is told a convincing local story fitted to their own loyalties.

## How the lore gets in
- **The Dealy Lama's Eris myth (about 1,500 words, a parable).** Five stoned Visions, each atrocity's author wearing Oliver Hardy's face and saying "Now look what you made me do". No stall: the Lama turns the Good/Evil frame upside down first, the listener stands on sore feet throughout, and he answers it all with "No".
- **Never Whistle While You're Pissing (about 900 words, a document read aloud).** The Snafu Principle and the redundancy passage, cut into a stoned game of "who is the Martian".
- **Mama Sutra's lecture (about 5,000 words, a conversation).** Bourbon is the gauge: she tells him to drink before each layer, and he goes from "Can't drink on duty" to "May I have more?" It sags in the Konan/Kukulan stretch (reported speech) but ends on Hagbard, which brings it back to the plot.
- **Telemachus Sneezed (about 1,800 words in two parts, a book summarised by a reader on a plane).** Atlanta Hope's novel has its own front reveal (mad Captain Cork secretly runs guns for God's Lightning), and its doomed pilot shares a name with the real pilot of Simon's plane. The clown version and the real thread are literally on the same flight.
- **Knight's memoir (about 4,500 words, a confession).** It keeps moving because each layer gets its own scene (banquet, riot, password).

## Mechanism notes
- **C (front), at full strength.** Three ways a layer breaks: a superior's offhand remark ("Nobody in the Order has ever told you the truth?"); a clearance or password detail; a fact the reader already holds (the Mohawk story told both ways). A layer holds for a scene (Mama Sutra) or for years (Knight). Each layer is delivered by someone who believes it.
- **Transfer for SDF (proposal).** The organisation's best move against James may be a Mama Sutra: a kind, credible person who already has his evidence and gives him a full counter-history in which the organisation is the careful, persecuted party and the leakers are cranks. James acts on it once (say, warns a helper off) and pays for it, then finds the crack himself in a small administrative detail (a distribution list, a "not cleared" note), never through a confession. It obeys the rules: it comes to him as an object, nobody is forced, and being wrong costs him.
- **A (gate), inverted.** Hagbard's own front is FUCKUP's "Assumption A", which lets him press the button and costs him the Demonstration it was meant to protect.
- **Comedy survives escalation** because each revelation comes in a comic voice, not because the stakes drop: Chips in a Lovecraft church, Danny's drink count, narcs named Galley and Eichmann "only following orders", the fifth set of strings. Hiroshima and the camps sit inside the Laurel-and-Hardy frame without being softened by it.
