# Philip K. Dick, *The Man in the High Castle* — questions an SDF agent will ask

> Answers point to the section notes in this folder (`01-chapters-1-to-6.md`, `02-chapters-7-to-12.md`, `03-chapters-13-to-15.md`) and, where relevant, to a v8.2 chapter by title. The map is `00-map.md`. Everything about SDF here is a PROPOSAL; Santi's decisions are PLACEHOLDER.

## The leak, in the novel

**1. What counts as a leak in this book?**
Five things: a banned novel in which the Allies won, a forged Colt, a plain pin with no past, the oracle, and a few minutes Tagomi spends in a different San Francisco. The first three are objects, the fourth is a text consulted by hand, and the fifth is an experience nobody else witnesses. See map section 4 and the "Mechanism notes" in all three section notes.

**2. Which leaks move the plot and which only color it?**
The Colt moves it hardest (ch. 4–6, `01-chapters-1-to-6.md`). The pin moves Childan and later Tagomi (ch. 11, 14). The banned novel moves it twice: the consul's burned order (ch. 8) and the trip to the author (ch. 10, 13; `02-chapters-7-to-12.md`). The oracle moves it once, in ch. 13 (`03-chapters-13-to-15.md`). Everything else colors.

**3. What separates a leak that pushes from one that colors?**
Whether it changes what someone signs, pays, drives to or burns. Tagomi's glimpse tells him nothing useful, but he signs a release because of it. The oracle tells Frink a lot and changes little. Map section 4, "The rule under all five."

**4. How is the banned novel delivered without stopping the story?**
It is retold in cars by people who disagree with it (ch. 5, about 900 words; ch. 10, about 700 words) and read in short excerpts that someone interrupts (ch. 8, ch. 10). Each scene is about the reader's reaction. The straight excerpts do stall, on purpose (`02-chapters-7-to-12.md`, "How the lore gets in").

**5. What does the forged Colt teach that the mystical leaks don't?**
That a fact can be true while its source is a fraud, and that acting on it can still ruin the wrong person. The caller is right about the gun and lying about himself; the forger then pays two innocent men (`01-chapters-1-to-6.md`, movement 2 and "Why it grips"). In SDF, Kalder's tip in Enjoy it while it lasts is already this: a true tip from a false friend. Map proposal 6.

**6. What is "historicity" and why does it matter to SDF?**
The idea that an object's history lives in its paperwork. The forger shows two identical lighters and says only the certificate separates them (ch. 5, `01-chapters-1-to-6.md`). SDF's organization works the same way: it controls catalogue cards, "withdrawn for conservation," the Outcome column in the visitor book. A thing with no paper is the one thing it cannot refile. Map section 4, "Paper against object."

**7. How does Tagomi's glimpse arrive?**
After he stops trying. He shakes the piece, curses it, tastes it, and gets nothing; the other city comes when he has given up (ch. 14, `03-chapters-13-to-15.md`). It lasts minutes, costs him fear and lost time, and nobody else sees it. SDF has the same rule in The night he stops being afraid (the fourth night).

**8. Who discredits the leaks?**
The people who receive them. Tagomi decides his senses slipped. The consul calls the novel cheap popular fiction while it is working on him (ch. 8). The author will not stand behind his book (ch. 15). The state bans the novel in one zone, but nobody in the book has to argue against it. SDF's James does the same at the funeral: "filed it under lack of sleep."

**9. Does the oracle ever tell anyone something they could not have reached alone?**
No, according to `01-chapters-1-to-6.md`, and the reader has no means to check a reading. Frink's reading says the money will not come and it does. This is why the map puts the oracle under "Do not take": James's method is checking.

**10. Why does the ending stay open?**
Juliana's throw says the book is true and the author answers that he is not sure of anything (ch. 15). The proof is given and refused. `03-chapters-13-to-15.md` says this keeps the dread and loses a payoff, because nothing in the German plot depends on it. ADDENDUM 05 already lists open endings under "does not work."

**11. What is the one direct claim that a leak is useful?**
The general says the banned novel will help Tagomi recover because it gives an outside frame of reference (ch. 12, `02-chapters-7-to-12.md`). That is the only time a character states what a fragment of another world is for.

**12. What happens when the enemy reads the leak?**
The consul is moved, resents it, writes an order to have the author killed, and burns the order out of fear of his superiors (ch. 8, `02-chapters-7-to-12.md`). For SDF this is a possible beat for Bram, who counts an experience he has never had (A room with a long table). The map names it and does not propose it, because James is not in that room.

## The engine

**13. Why can no character stop?**
Each has acted on something uncheckable before the chapter starts: Frink on half an oracle answer, Childan on a stranger's tip, the forger on a guess about who is blackmailing him. See the engine table in the map and the "Movements" tables in each note.

**14. What is the most efficient scene?**
The phone call to the newspaper desk in ch. 4. Three lines turn a large sale into an impostor nobody can explain (`01-chapters-1-to-6.md`). The facts do not resolve, and Childan has to act anyway.

**15. How does the conspiracy proper (the first-strike plan) move things?**
It is background pressure in chapters 1–6, the driver of chapters 7–12 (the SD must reach Baynes before his message reaches Tokyo), and in 13–15 it is spent: it shapes each life and lets it go (`01`, `02`, `03`, "How the conspiracy moves the story here"). The mystery that carries the ending is a different one: who wrote the book.

**16. How does the reader learn the assassin's identity?**
By the same small slips the heroine sees: he knew a new shirt needed ironing, the hotel had valet service, he dyed his hair (ch. 13, `03-chapters-13-to-15.md`). The note calls this fairer than any file or confession. It matches the brief's rule that James gets there by dumb small questions.

**17. How does a document change a life in this book?**
Twice. Childan signs a police statement that "ends your involvement" and puts Frink on file (ch. 7, `02-chapters-7-to-12.md`). Tagomi signs Frink's release when asked to hand him over (ch. 14, `03-chapters-13-to-15.md`). Both men sign without knowing who Frink is to them. Compare Anneke's signature in Monday.

**18. Is there a gate (A)?**
A small one. Childan has to know whether the gun is real before he can decide whom to ruin; the lab answers, and the answer makes things worse (ch. 4, `01-chapters-1-to-6.md`). This supports ADDENDUM 05's "cost after the gate."

**19. Is there a canary (B)?**
Only inverted: the caller plants a false story about himself and watches (ch. 4). `02-chapters-7-to-12.md` says there is none in chapters 7–12. The map does not build on it.

**20. Is there a front (C) and a key (D)?**
Fronts: the forgery business inside a foundry (`01`), and a cover identity whose stated purpose is to make the other side go through formalities (ch. 12, `02`). Keys: a clerk's name that reaches a contact in six minutes (ch. 10, `02`), and the hexagram Juliana and the author both know without the chart (ch. 15, `03`). The second is the model for PLACEHOLDER #5 in The math.

## The leak, tested against SDF

**21. What in SDF already reads as a leak?**
The flash in The funeral (cold mud, a cracked hand). The hand seen "all my life" and the lives cut off "by a man with a list" in v6 The floor. The job name `rcl-agg`, the invoice's "recall index," and "recall" in Utrecht, 1993. Henk Boersma's "strange things about his father" (A pension visit, Zwolle). Ana's dream of cracked hands in v6 Coda, which v8 Epilogue dropped. And the line James says to Els in The math.

**22. Would a leak mechanism repair REVIEW 01 finding 1.1?**
Yes. If *given* means "received from before" and *arose* means "you made it up," the edit is a discredit applied to a sentence. History, instruction and "the passing" become the same thing. Map section 5.

**23. Would it repair 1.2 (the hidden thing is on sale)?**
Mostly. Leaks happen in people and cannot be deleted, only refiled, which fits the review's own fix (make it exotic or funny). The forty-one then are the dry books where an author reported a leak plainly.

**24. Would it repair 1.3 (the motive)?**
Only with one more decision. A leak gives the organization a reason to care if what people recall includes the organization, "a man with a list" in v6 The floor. Then every leak is testimony against it. That decision is Santi's, PLACEHOLDER, and Kalder still needs a line.

**25. Where does the leak idea break a rule?**
Five places, listed in map section 5: a third leak to James makes him a seer; an object from before a reset breaks the real-world rule and the book that claims nothing; "all forty-one are one edit" does not fit entries 3, 7 and 17 of The List; the count registers only what is said (v7 The payload); and a leak cannot go on a sheet as a claim.

**26. Can James have more leaks than the funeral and the floor?**
The map says no. The brief forbids him out-deducing the book, and insights are meant to happen to him slowly. Further leaks should land in Els, Anneke, Ana or the widow and reach him as their words. Proposal 2 is flagged as the exception and the risk.

**27. Can a physical object leak from an earlier world?**
Not as a confirmed thing. Urfa, 1994 is careful ("the book never says he found it"), and REVIEW 01 1.4 found the reset already cut from the plan. Proposal 4 puts an invented plain object on the table in The final conversation and requires that only the old man says what it is.

**28. How would the organization respond to a leak?**
As it does now: agree, then reclassify (Els's folder in The math). Under proposal 1 the forum edit in The gorilla mask becomes that response to a leak James sent out on purpose. The 1931 method was to edit books; the present method is to count and refile people. Killing stays rare.

**29. How does a leak join the nightly count?**
v7 The payload lists verbs of asking (posted, wrote, asked, searched, requested, enquired, raised). Proposal 1 adds one verb, *recalled*, which James produces by posting the hand and reading the next night's file. The opening's "counted the night you asked" stays true.

**30. How does a leak join the win?**
Through paper only. Proposal 3 puts a week of the reason column, numbers removed, on Anneke's sheet in The sheets, so "per flagged person" has content, and she finds her own row. That keeps the sheets as diffs and gives her refusal to sign in Monday a cause of her own. It also answers REVIEW 01 2.1.

**31. What is the check for a leak, given James's method is two copies?**
Two people with the same thing and no route between them. The plan already says "the reports rhyme" (Reading with a pen). Proposal 5 gives the book one such pair, James and Ana, and has him enter it in the thin book and say nothing.

**32. Does this map confirm or contradict ADDENDUM 05?**
It confirms the rule on lore, the cost after the gate, and the verdict on open endings. It adds three mechanisms (paper against object, the wrong payee, the enemy as reader). It contradicts nothing, but it puts a limit on "reveals are objects": in this novel the object alone proves nothing, and a person or a paper has to vouch.

## Practical

**33. What must not be copied?**
Real people as perpetrators in an invented history; slurs in the characters' heads; a cover identity used to kill; a statement signed under police pressure; the shooting and the cut neck. See the Flags in all three notes and the map's standing-rule note.

**34. Do the section notes disagree anywhere?**
On small things. `01` says the novel "weakly pushes," `02` counts two pushes, `03` says it does not move the German plot; these fit together but read differently. `02` heads its oracle paragraph "ch. 9, 12" and then cites ch. 10. `03` has Tagomi holding the piece on a bench and also says it was left on the grass. `03` does not say who speaks the "sound asleep" line, and its reader had not read chapters 1–12.

**35. What did the map add from memory?**
One thing, marked: that the silver piece Tagomi holds is Frink's work and neither man learns of the link. The notes give both halves (the Edfrank pin in `02`, the signed release in `03`) and do not join them.

**36. Which proposal should be tried first?**
Proposal 1 (The payload → The gorilla mask). It is one posted sentence and one new row, it makes an existing plan beat (the forum edit) into a consequence of something James did, and it shows the reader an edit removing a leak without anyone explaining it.
