# Blake Crouch, *Recursion* — questions an SDF agent will ask

> Answers point to the section notes in this folder (`01-book-one.md`, `02-middle.md`, `03-books-three-to-five.md`) and, where relevant, to a v8.2 chapter by title. The map is `00-map.md`. The text has no chapter numbers, so cites are by Book, date or loop. Everything about SDF here is a PROPOSAL; Santi's decisions are PLACEHOLDER.

## The leak itself

**1. What is a leak in this novel, in one sentence?**
When someone restarts time from an old memory, the stretch they abandoned comes back to everyone who lived it as a second, gray set of memories. Helena is told she will have one live set and one dead (`01-book-one.md`, Mechanism notes and Quotes).

**2. What is the tell?**
A nosebleed and a stab of pain, then the memories (`01-book-one.md`; `03-books-three-to-five.md` adds the headache behind the eyes). It is the same every time, so characters and the reader can recognize it in someone else. For SDF see map 5.1: the flash in The funeral has no repeatable sign yet.

**3. How does the fixed date work?**
The dead memories arrive at the moment the earlier self died or left, which Helena names a timeline anniversary (`02-middle.md`). In Book Five the date is 16 April 2019 for everyone alive (`03-books-three-to-five.md`). The date lets characters wait for the leak and prepare, and lets Crouch write the dread before the event.

**4. Who is the second witness, and why does it matter?**
Barry's first life arrives in Julia and Meghan on the same night, so he cannot file it as his own illness (`02-middle.md`, leak 2). Earlier, Gwen's CDC detail that the syndrome spreads between acquaintances does the same job (`01-book-one.md`). In SDF the candidate is the widow in The other ten (map 5.1).

**5. What is the checkable contradiction?**
Ann says Franny jumped; the police report says Joe saved her. A detective can settle that with a train ride (`01-book-one.md`, Movements step 1 and the lore list). The leak is handed to someone whose job is to settle that kind of disagreement, which is also James's job at the bank.

**6. How does a leak carry usable evidence?**
Dead memories hold a route, a gun position and a code, and Helena proves herself to Barry with his eclipse, his orchard and his father (`02-middle.md`, leaks 4 and 5 and "Evidence, not feeling"). The memory is read like a document. Map 5.2 gives James the SDF version: a sentence he can test for a source.

**7. Why do the leaked lives feel real?**
They come in small physical details: a birthmark, a cake fight, morning breath (`01-book-one.md`, Ann). Barry's own first memory is built sense by sense, smell first (`01-book-one.md`, Why it grips). v6 The floor already agrees: the lives are short, ordinary and "boring."

**8. Does a leak always cost the person who has it?**
Yes. Ann dies, Reed hangs himself, Meghan cannot hold two lives, and Slade ends with only flashes (`02-middle.md`). `03-books-three-to-five.md` notes that the costs are bodily and relational, never abstract.

## Escalation

**9. How does Crouch use one device for a whole book?**
He changes the scale each time: one person, a family, a city, a nation, the world at once, one morning rewritten four times, and last an older mind arriving in a younger body (`02-middle.md`, "How it escalates without repeating").

**10. What is the SDF ladder, and how much exists?**
Most of it. The funeral (one man), The math (the professor said the same line), The other ten (a dead stranger), Leiden, nine years ago (the handler, seen only by the reader), The sheets (the quiet ones, by password), The final conversation (remembering as policy), and the Epilogue (Ida at the stairs). The missing rungs are the second and third, which map 5.1 and 5.2 supply.

**11. What is the "save point," and can SDF use it?**
Helena cuts her arm to make a short vivid memory she can return to (`02-middle.md`, leak 5). No. It needs the chair, and it turns the leak into a tool the hero controls. Rule 9 of the v8 brief says insights happen to James.

**12. Where does the escalation fail?**
In the last third. `03-books-three-to-five.md` says anniversaries two and five are almost the same nuclear strike. Each loop changes place and genre, but the reader has learned the pattern.

**13. How do the loops stay readable at all?**
Five ways, listed in `03-books-three-to-five.md`: new setting and companions each time, uneven knowledge between the couple, costs that deepen, one restart shown in full and the rest compressed, and characters who name their own fatigue.

## Lore and rules

**14. How does Crouch explain the rules without stalling?**
Each rule arrives when a character needs it to act in the next minute: whisky after a death, a phone call cut off, a family in a bathroom at three in the morning (`01-book-one.md`, `02-middle.md`). Nobody asks how it works before the reader has seen it work.

**15. Where does the lore stall?**
Slade's first long explanation to Helena, about 2,000 words (`01-book-one.md`), and the neutrino and wormhole pages (`03-books-three-to-five.md`). The second pays off nothing, since the answer is a decision. ADDENDUM 05's rule holds: nobody in the room can lose by what is being said.

**16. Which lore scene should I study for Frank's kitchen or Leiden?**
Helena's kitchen in Book Three (`02-middle.md`, about 2,500 words): the future is told over a meal she cooks from his own memories, so every fact is also proof. Then Slade through the glass in loop six (`03-books-three-to-five.md`), where the listener must unlock a door to hear the rest.

**17. Is there a document that works as a reveal?**
The police report, about 300 words (`01-book-one.md`), and Helena's DVD in loop seven (`03-books-three-to-five.md`), an object in Barry's hands that asks him to pause it. Both fit SDF's rule that reveals are objects.

## The conspiracy

**18. Is there an organization at all?**
Barely. First a hotel that invites the desperate, then a state program, then nobody, once the design is public (`01-book-one.md`, `02-middle.md`). `03-books-three-to-five.md` says the secret by then is only the chair and Slade's workaround. It is a poor model for SDF's helpers and a good one for a single device.

**19. How does the conspiracy treat the investigator?**
It invites him, threatens once, then gives him what he wants most and asks him to keep quiet (`01-book-one.md`, "It makes the detective a client"). SDF has the same move in the five seats of The reading room, continued, which James refuses.

**20. What is Shaw's argument, and does SDF have it?**
Each use of the chair saves lives, so refusing looks like harm (`02-middle.md`). SDF has it in Anneke's reason in The final conversation: the truth would collapse the world. No change proposed.

**21. What does the book say about A, B, C and D?**
A is weak (five rules, a synaptic number). B appears once. C is three layers that are only learned. D is the strongest: private facts open Barry, and Slade's key is held until his cell is unlocked (all three notes, "Fixes"). See map section 4.

**22. Are there mechanisms not in ADDENDUM 05?**
Two. The dated arrival, a hidden thing that surfaces by itself on a known day (`02-middle.md`). And the perishable finding: what the hero learns is lost at each restart and survives only through a person (`03-books-three-to-five.md`). The gift as a lock is the addendum's co-optation with a clearer price.

## The leak in SDF

**23. Does SDF already have a leak?**
Yes, of one kind. The funeral has the flash of cold mud and a cracked hand, The night he stops being afraid pays it, v6 The floor lists *remembered → imagined* among the edits, and the old man says in The final conversation that some of them remember across lives. What is missing is anything James can do with it before the floor.

**24. Does the organization already have a word for it?**
"Recall." The foundation man uses it in Utrecht, 1993, the nightly job is `rcl-agg` in Leave it, and the invoice in The money bills a "recall index." No change is needed to make the count a count of people who recall; someone only has to say the word in front of James (map 5.1).

**25. Should the right column change to "people who are leaking"?**
No. A room with a long table defines it as people whose fear is leaving, judged by handlers who have crossed. Keep that and make the leak the sign a handler looks for. Changing the definition would touch Bram's scenes, The long table, again and the opening.

**26. What leaks, to whom, in the proposals?**
A memory with a bodily sign, to Henk and to James (map 5.1, The other ten). A sentence, to James and the professor (5.2, The math). Dated flashes, laid out for Ana (5.3, Twenty minutes). The same sentence, corrected by the old man (5.4, The reading room, continued). No object leaks.

**27. Why may an object not leak?**
Because SDF's proof is two printings compared. If things can change by themselves, a changed sentence no longer needs an editor, and Leiden, 1931 loses its point. `01-book-one.md` flags that Crouch uses the Mandela Effect this way; `02-middle.md` has a building that appears in a city.

**28. Should SDF use other realities as well as past lives?**
No. Crouch has one kind of leak and one tell, and it is the reason the reader can follow. The earlier world reaches SDF through people who carried it, which is who the givers were.

**29. How does the organization answer a leak?**
It discredits. *When a Reader Notices* in The math already says agree and reclassify, and ADDENDUM 05's change 3 gives James a wellbeing referral. The thermostat in The band already funds the exotic version, so the ordinary one sounds like it. Nothing new needs building.

**30. Does the leak repair REVIEW 01 finding 1.1?**
Yes, if it is one kind. What was given was the method of carrying across, the givers were the ones who carried it, and "the thing that passes" is the carrying. Map 5.2 puts that in James's hands as a sentence before Part Three.

**31. Does it repair 1.2?**
Partly. A leak is ordinary, personal and unasked for, which no published book of the dead is. That supports the review's reading that the edits remove the ordinary versions. Noor still has to say so in Leiden.

**32. Does it repair 1.3?**
Mostly. A person who recalls is a witness to the world before the record was arranged, which gives the organization a selfish reason to find them. It has to arrive on paper before Part Three, or the old man hands over the answer the way Slade does (`03-books-three-to-five.md`). Kalder's line is still missing.

**33. What must a book about resets not do?**
Explain for more than a page without a scene break, make a restart free, make the fix a piece of technology, show the same catastrophe twice, or give the solution to the villain (`03-books-three-to-five.md`, the list in Mechanism notes). For SDF add: never show the reset.

**34. Would a public leak on a date work in SDF?**
No. Leave it promises that the date passes and nothing loud happens, and the machine counts people one at a time. Keep dates private: 02:14 on the oven clock, and the log in map 5.3.

**35. Does the leak make James special?**
It must not. In Crouch everyone gets it. In SDF the professor, Henk and the quiet ones in The sheets share the sentence, and the password scene depends on that.

## Rules and limits

**36. What in the book breaks SDF's rules?**
Real organizations and people named (`02-middle.md`, Flags), an armed raid and a kidnapping (`02-middle.md`, `03-books-three-to-five.md`), a machine as the cause, and a middle with no comedy. `01-book-one.md` notes that Gwen's banter is the only relief.

**37. Is there anything for the dog?**
No dog in the notes. The nearest thing is the ending: Barry keeps the memory of someone who does not know him and sits down beside her (`03-books-three-to-five.md`). The Epilogue's Ida at the stairs is SDF's version, already planned.

**38. Do the three notes disagree anywhere?**
In one place. `03-books-three-to-five.md` puts the phone call with Jee-woon in loop four in its Movements table and in loop five in its "do not" list. Also, Slade dies in Book Three (`02-middle.md`) and is alive in a cell in loop six (`03-books-three-to-five.md`); that is a new timeline, though the note does not say so. `03` was written without the first two thirds.
