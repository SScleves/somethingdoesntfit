# Addendum 05 — Conspiracy engines (what moves a conspiracy plot)
### 11 Nov 2026. After Tier 1 (five of seven books; *The Club Dumas* and *Six Days of the Condor* not yet downloaded). Will be redone after Tier 2.

> Sources: the five maps and Q&As in `08 Research/Engines/` (King, Borges, Eco, Orwell, Gibson), checked against THE BOOK v8.2, the v8 brief, the v7 Part Two drafts (19, 23, 24, 27, 30, 41, 43, 45) and `01 Manuscript/v6/18 — On land.md`, plus v6 drafts where a map's claim needed it. Everything here is a PROPOSAL. Your decisions are marked PLACEHOLDER.

The problem, restated so the rest makes sense: in Part Two James receives paper. Nothing he must prove before he acts, and nothing he does can go wrong except by frightening him. The five books agree on the fix in different words. The reason the hero cannot stop should become something *he* did: a lie, a bait, a signature, a choice of where to stand. Eco's map shows the switch happening in the engine table at chapter 75; Part Two needs the same switch, and the earlier it comes the better.

## a. The mechanisms, one by one

**A. The gate.** *Supported by:* King (strongly), Eco (as a gate inside the canary), Borges (gates belief, not action), Orwell (only the dial, III.ii). Gibson is weak. *Best example:* King, *11/22/63*, ch. 3 and ch. 7. Al's "ninety-five percent isn't a hundred" sets the bar, and Derry adds that knowledge loses its value once you use it, so the gate controls *when* Jake may act as well as *what* he must know. What makes the wait bearable is the calendar (Oswald abroad until June 1962), not the doubt. *Fits SDF?* Yes, if James sets the gate himself, the schedule's dates do the holding, and the gate opens because a person chooses to answer (Frank), never at gunpoint as King's does (ch. 25). *Verdict:* use it. It is the named gap.

**B. The canary.** *Supported by:* Eco (central), Gibson (Keiko, and it bites back), Orwell (run on the hero), Borges (the hrönir rule: the test fails if the tested know it is a test). *Best example:* Eco, *Foucault's Pendulum*, ch. 75–76: one fake sect planted in a real chronology; Agliè asks who mentioned it, asks again, checks his watch. The tell is the second question. *Fits SDF?* Yes, with an invented title only, never a real book. The plan already has it once (On land), but as written it proves only that Kalder bluffs. Eco says what it is missing: a unique item, a hook someone can act on, a readout through a third person, and a price. *Verdict:* use it, and give the existing one those four parts before you add a second.

**C. The front.** *Supported by:* Borges (Uqbar is a front for Tlön), Orwell (the Brotherhood for the Party), Gibson (layered, then deflated). Eco and King are weak. *Best example:* Orwell, *1984*, III.iii: "I wrote it." *Fits SDF?* As structure it is already there: the archive on top of the recovery site, and the helpers' tiers on Kalder's phone (honeypot, disaster recovery, conservation, heritage). *Verdict:* in Tier 1 it does not work as a Part Two engine. No map could turn it into something James *does* in a named chapter; in every book it is something the hero *learns*. Leave it to *Illuminatus!* in Tier 2 to prove otherwise.

**D. Lock and key.** *Supported by:* King (private facts as keys), Gibson (a key with a price), Orwell (the rhyme as countersign), Eco and Borges (small; Borges's is the key the narrator let drop). *Best example:* Gibson, *Pattern Recognition*, ch. 29–31: Baranov trades the address for a Curta bought with Bigend's money, and that tie comes due. *Fits SDF?* Already on the page: Groningen turned by one question, Els's line, Noor's shelfmark, the forum password in The sheets. *Verdict:* nothing to build. Take only Gibson's point that a key paid for with someone else's credit sends a bill.

**New mechanisms the maps name, with verdicts:**

- **The cost that lands after the gate.** King ch. 9 (Ellen's call: "Where were you then"), ch. 26–29: being right does not make the act safe. *Fits:* yes. It is the second half of A and must travel with it.
- **Checks as the engine.** Borges, part I: every check comes back blank in the same direction, and the blanks are the plot. *Fits:* already on the page (The sentence, The control case). What Borges adds is a price on every check (a library request in his own name). That belongs in Part One, so it is not in the table.
- **True bait, "why could I find it?"** Orwell II.ix, III.iii: the forbidden book is accurate, and reading it identifies the reader. *Fits:* yes; the opening already says "we leave them findable." Missing: James asking the question before he opens the annex.
- **The hero does the machine's homework.** Orwell I.iv (Ogilvy): Winston forges well and is proud of it. *Fits:* yes, and it is the cleanest fix for The money: his initials on the machine's paperwork, a helper by his own hand.
- **The side channel that carries the bill.** King ch. 23–25: the bets that paid for the wait bring Roth's men. *Fits:* yes. The friendship with Kalder is the channel; the dog is the bill.
- **The invention that can't be taken back.** Eco ch. 76 (the lie about a source becomes a debt) and ch. 118 (denying it makes it bigger). *Fits:* yes, as the price of any canary James sets.
- Smaller ones worth keeping in the drawer: Orwell's restored tell (III.iv, the dust put back); Gibson's old post as the tripwire (ch. 41) and holding back as an action (ch. 20); Eco's frame (ch. 108–109, the prober's own habits turned into evidence); Orwell's broken man left in public (III.vi). *Does not work:* Gibson's deflated conspiracy (ch. 41), and Borges's withdrawal ending. James publishes.

## c. The three changes I would make first

**1. The money: James does the machine's homework (Orwell 1 and 2).** It fixes the exact chapter the diagnosis names, it costs one scene with Saskia, and its cost is permanent: his initials are on the seat annex. What it breaks: **The drill** (the role must be granted between the drill and the money, so Saskia's "see if anything's expired" changes); **Enjoy it while it lasts** (the narrowed access now needs the blank "granted by" line, or King 5 if you want Saskia to pay); **The reading room, continued** (the old man gets a line of thanks, and his "we leave them findable" stops being abstract); **The sheets** (James's own sheet joins Pieter's); **The dinner** is untouched. Drop Orwell's "read (annex)" payload line: the job vanished in The week it vanished, so James can no longer read payloads.

**2. On land: a canary with a hook and a readout (Eco 5.1, with Orwell 3's one line and Eco 5.4 as its negative in The band).** It turns a plan-only bluff into a test that reports back, through a helper who chooses to talk, and it gives Part Two its one costly wrong result. What it breaks: **On land** has to be written fresh (v6 is the other story); **Groningen** must leave the dealer reachable and willing; **The schedule** (is the dealer on it?); **The dinner** ("the book you said you'd read" now has a shelfmark, which strengthens it); **The sheets** (the shelfmark appears in Aylin's visitor book, which in v7 already has a "Shelfmark requested" column); **The gorilla mask** (if "Deventer" moves to the pub, as the brief says, On land carries two slips and gets crowded; see d). One bait only: Eco 5.3's forum bait would make neither readable.

**3. James's own gate, held from Leiden to Frank, loose, and tested at the eighth address (King 4 and 1).** This is the gate the pipeline found missing: something James must prove before he may act, with a calendar pushing against it and a cost when he waits. What it breaks: **Leiden** gains a written rule; **The other ten** loses its ending on not acting and gains a watch that fails; **The reading room, continued** must use "nobody came because you'd gone" (already in v7) as the old man's answer to it; **Frank, loose** becomes a hinge, so its placement after The band matters; **The sheets** cannot start before Frank, loose; and the clock at the end of Part Two ("the review is two weeks away") has one fewer week if the ninth date has passed.

Next in line, if you want a fourth: The gorilla mask as the side channel (King 3). It is one paragraph, and it makes the poisoning something James paid for.

## d. Corrections

**Where the maps were wrong or redundant, now that the drafts have been read:**

- **Eco 5.5 is out of order.** It has Pieter test James with "recall" at the drill, "on the invoice he has read." In v8.2 The drill comes before The money, so he hasn't read it yet. Fixed in the table by using the job name.
- **Orwell 1 is half on the page.** v7 The money already has "Dependants per allocation: 3. He has two children." Only the task, the correction and the initials are new. **Orwell 2's cost is impossible as written**: the payload job is gone from Part One on.
- **King 6 understates the draft.** v6 The vet already has James conclude Noor went dark "because I'd been to her house, with my face." New is only the person who tells him and the change of method.
- **King 1 is half drafted.** v7 The other ten holds the dates back and promises not to "knock on that door with a dog"; v7 The reading room, continued has "I was there. Nobody came. Why." New is stating it as a test, in advance, with a stake.
- **Gibson 5.2's cost repeats Part One.** The control case already has James lie to Ana about what a copy cost. Not used.
- **The depot's reference format arrives three times in the plan**: Noor says so in Leiden; The drill says he notes it and "doesn't connect it" (v7 The drill connects it on the last page); Noor hands him the shelfmark again in The vet (v6). Pick one. Eco 5.1 needs James to know the format by On land.
- **v7 Frank, loose** gives Frank children ("not with Frank, who had children"); the brief makes him twenty-nine and single. It also dates the invoice step twice, once to the stand-up and once to the ticket.
- **Borges 1 and 5** (the missing first impression, the index fossil) are Part One proposals (The control case) and sit outside this table. Worth a separate look.
