# Comp — Peter Watts, *Blindsight* / *Visión ciega* (2006; Spain-Spanish tr. Manuel de los Reyes, Bibliópolis 2009)

> **Read 100%**, 11 Oct 2026, in the Spanish edition, start to finish including the acknowledgements and the fifteen pages of notes and references (~110k words). Brief: **voice (the 10% Siri), the thesis mirror, present/past pace, the ending.** Standing rule: in the novel *Blindsight* is quoted at phrase length only; this comp locates the passage the professor's four words sit beside so the phrase can be chosen. Quotes below are Spanish as printed, under 15 words, English gloss in brackets. Locations are line offsets in the vault's .txt (8,857 lines); the edition has no page numbers.

## 1. What it is

Siri Keeton had half his brain removed at eight for epilepsy and rebuilt himself by watching: *Observé, grabé, derivé los algoritmos y reproduje las conductas apropiadas* [I observed, recorded, derived the algorithms, reproduced the right behaviours] (250). As an adult he is a *sinteticista*, a paid conduit who translates post-human specialists for the people who fund them without understanding the content; a Chinese room that knows it is one. In 2082, 65,536 probes photograph the Earth and burn. A ship, Theseus, crewed by four augmented specialists and commanded by a resurrected vampire, Sarasti, is sent after the signal and finds Rorschach: a city-sized artefact growing in orbit around a dark superjovian, inhabited by *trepadores* (scramblers) that are faster and smarter than any human and not conscious. The book's argument is that consciousness is an expensive evolutionary accident and intelligence runs better without it. Sarasti tortures Siri into believing it, then sends him home as the one witness who can make Earth believe. Siri narrates from a lifeboat, waking one hour in ten thousand, falling toward an Earth that may already belong to the vampires.

Structure: a prologue (the playground), *Teseo* (7 chapters), *Rorschach* (15), *Caribdis* (1), then notes. There are no past chapters. The past (Pag, the mother in digital heaven, the absent father, Chelsea) enters inside present chapters at section breaks and leaves on a one-line price. Every chapter opens with an epigraph (Hemingway, Ted Bundy, Suzanne Vega, Jesus of Nazareth).

## 2. The mechanisms worth stealing

### 2a. The method on the page: a line of dialogue, then one line of what it meant.
Watts states the method once, early: *si se ignoraba la carne y se estudiaba la topología* [if you ignored the flesh and studied the topology] (323). Then he never explains it again; he performs it, in three registers.

- **A tell that is a number.** The father calls during a planetary crisis. Siri notices the pauses, tugs the line and counts: *Mil uno, mil dos...* [one thousand one, one thousand two] (721-727). Three seconds of lag. *—Estás en la Luna —dije.* [You're on the Moon] (729). He infers position from latency without ever seeing inside. That is the observability engineer's one skill in a single beat, and it is domestic, not technical: a son reading his father's silences. James's first inference should have exactly this shape.
- **Double-track dialogue.** With Bates (2948-2978) the dialogue is formatted as dialogue and each of her answers is followed by what she meant, in guillemets: *—Nadie nos ha disparado todavía —respondió. Queriendo decir: «Ni de coña».* [Nobody has shot us yet, she said. Meaning: not a chance] (2970). *—Tiene sentido, supongo. —«Gilipolleces».* [Makes sense, I suppose. Bullshit] (2974). And at 3100, *Lo leí en ella:* [I read it in her] followed by three unsaid lines in quotes. One line of subtext after a line of speech; never a paragraph of psychology. This is rule 9 (dialogue over monologue) with the reading done in the margin.
- **The verdict without a receipt.** Siri notices a medical-record access, reads one value, concludes there is an 82% chance James has become too trusting for Sarasti, and adds: *No tenía ni idea de cómo sabía yo eso. Nunca la tenía.* [I had no idea how I knew that. I never did] (4855). Later: *Ninguna de las dos partes era capaz de discernir de dónde habían salido* [neither half could tell where the findings had come from] (4521). Cunningham names the mechanism: the subconscious modules *Le pasa notas por debajo de la mesa* [pass notes under the table] (7561). That is "insights happen to him," rule 10, as a working method: the inference arrives, hedged, smaller than the evidence, and the receipt comes later or not at all.

**The observer's blind spot, which is James's flaw.** Watts gives it a mechanism in three places. Sarasti: *Siri es el mejor en su campo. Pero no cuando le toca de cerca.* [Siri is the best in his field. But not when it touches him] (6031). Siri on Cunningham: *También tenía que imaginarme que era Siri Keeton* [I also had to imagine I was Siri Keeton] (6623), after a page admitting *estaba modelando el sistema equivocado* [I was modelling the wrong system] (6605). And Sarasti's diagnosis at 8181: a man who believes he has no opinions must assign every opinion he feels to someone else, *La mitad de nosotros somos tú. Creo que el término es «proyección».* [Half of us are you. I believe the term is projection]. Siri "sees" a mutiny Bates never plans. James stops trusting people before he has finished needing them; Watts shows how a reader of surfaces manufactures the betrayal he then reads. That is the engine of the Kalder friendship and of the Ana misreading, and it costs nothing to borrow because it is structural, not textural.

### 2b. First person carrying hard explanation: confess the lecture, or have someone push for it.
- **Confess it.** *Supongo que debería explicarlo, ahora que es completamente irrelevante.* [I suppose I should explain it, now that it is completely irrelevant] (1617) opens a page on the three tribes of first-contact theory. The frame turns the lecture into a man talking to himself in a coffin; the content is allowed because the voice has already admitted it is beside the point. James's version is the dogs: he explains because the listener can't stop him.
- **Explain the job as a wound.** *Esto es lo que mi padre no podía deshacer. Esto es lo que soy:* [This is what my father couldn't undo. This is what I am:] (823), then the only full account of what a synthesist does. "I work in IT" with depth only when the story forces it: Watts forces it with a father's apology.
- **Let the other person land the counter.** The Chinese room (2576-2626) is explained to Chelsea in four short turns because she asks *¿cómo puedes traducir algo si no lo comprendes?* [how can you translate what you don't understand] (2582); she then scores the point (*en realidad tú no entiendes el cantonés* [you really don't understand Cantonese], 2612), and he deflects to sex. Ana gets the explanation, lands the counter, and James deflects to the dogs.
- **The one straight lecture that works** is the catalogue of neurological cases (4531-4551), and it works because it is a frightened man reading case files at night to find out what happened to his crewmate. That is fourteen tabs at 2 a.m., and the paragraph ends on the room, not the theory: *Dentro de Rorschach no había sitio para ellas.* [Inside Rorschach there was no room for them] (4551).
- **The fragments** (Voice Rules 8, ~30% texture). What is worth taking is not the run-on sentence but the one-word paragraph that pays a long one: *Huesudo.* [Bony] (2518); *Abajo.* [Down] (7027); *Encantador. Ésa era la palabra. Encantador.* (1867). Long paragraph, then the single word that names what the paragraph was circling.

### 2c. The deflection engine, with the price shown.
Siri deflects by turning the question around (*Cuéntame qué se siente* [tell me what it's like], 1279, after *Ea. Eso debería ampliar las distancias* [There. That should widen the distance], 1275), by changing the subject to the body (2618), and by reflex: *evitando sus preguntas en un acto reflejo* [dodging her questions by reflex] (6733). What makes it usable is that Watts always shows the bill. The argument with Chelsea about manipulation (4939-5003) ends with her calling him a worm and the narrator's *Misión cumplida.* [Mission accomplished] (5003). Her exit ends: *Era tan condenadamente bueno, que lo hacía incluso sin proponérmelo.* [I was so damned good I did it without meaning to] (6111). The novel's recipe keeps the engine and the self-knowledge and drops the victory: James deflects and loses; Ana leaves over an address, not because he won.

The register recipe, exact. Siri writes Chelsea a bedtime story about sperm and egg at war, brings flowers, notes that flowers are *los genitales amputados de otra especie* [the amputated genitals of another species] (1906), recites it as they're about to have sex, and closes: *Todavía no sé qué es lo que salió mal.* [I still don't know what went wrong] (1908). Serious observation, concrete detail, joke that is a confession. Keep the shape; strip the biology.

### 2d. Where the coldness lives, so it can be excluded.
The coldness is never cruelty; it is an absent feeling reported accurately. The playground: *Ni siquiera me apetecía especialmente echarle una mano.* [I didn't even particularly feel like helping him] (174). Leaving his parents' last visit: *Podría dejarles toda una puta vida entera a solas.* [I could leave them alone a whole fucking lifetime] (493). Pag calls: *—Me alegro de verte —dije, porque eso era lo que decía la gente* [Good to see you, I said, because that's what people said] (1063). After Szpindel's death: *Debería llorarlo.* [I should mourn him] (4665). *¿Crees que debería contarle que a veces me estremezco con sólo mirarla?* [Should I tell her that sometimes I shudder just looking at her?] (5457). Chelsea dying on video, Siri searching ten thousand deathbed transcripts for the right one: *Sencillamente, no lograba encontrar el algoritmo apropiado.* [I simply couldn't find the right algorithm] (6993).

Exclude all of that. But note where the 10% actually sits: the three places Siri's engine slips. *Esta mujer me gusta.* [I like this woman] (1305). *Quería que me abrazara eternamente.* [I wanted her to hold me forever] (3905). *Dios, cómo atesoro su carta.* [God, how I treasure his letter] (8570). James has the feeling and reports it; his problem is trusting it, not having it. "Siri's method with Santi's heart" is Watts's own slips made the default.

### 2e. The thesis mirror: where the "nobody there" argument is, and what a man who disagreed would underline.
**Location.** Part *Rorschach*, chapter 12, the page immediately after Sarasti drives a blade through Siri's hand. In the vault's .txt: **lines 7161-7195**. It begins with Siri's forced un-selfing, *Sarasti ni siquiera existía ya. No existía nadie.* [Sarasti didn't exist any more. Nobody existed] (7161), and runs as a sermon Siri says he can only report as heard (7167). Its spine: the body moves before the "I" decides, so *Tú no estás al mando* [You are not in charge] (7175); consciousness is *una muleta* [a crutch] (7183); *Los metaprocesos se extienden como el cáncer, y despiertan, y se hacen llamar «yo».* [The metaprocesses spread like cancer, and wake, and call themselves "I"] (7189); *El yo es un desperdicio de energía y capacidad de proceso* [The self is a waste of energy and processing] (7193); and when the lower processes say "I don't exist," *sólo están informando de la muerte de los parásitos* [they are only reporting the death of the parasites] (7195). The four pencilled words belong beside 7189-7195. Phrase-length quote available for the novel: *se hacen llamar «yo»* or *No existía nadie.*

**Seeds and echoes**, for the professor's list: Rorschach's taunt *Creéis que no hay nadie aquí, ¿verdad?* [You think there's nobody here, don't you?] (2754), the Chinese-room argument thrown back by the alien; Bates's Cotard episode, *No estoy aquí fuera... en ninguna parte... No soy nada* (4025-4047); the zombie conversation (7329-7377) in which Cunningham asks *¿cuántos sociópatas aparecen en los escalafones más altos del mundo* [how many sociopaths turn up in the world's top ranks] (7363), which is a Keeper line Watts gives to a biologist; "imagine you are a scrambler" (7721-7755), ending *La señal es un ataque.* [The signal is an attack]; Bates's *¿Qué diferencia hay entre estar muerto y sencillamente desconocer que estás vivo?* [What's the difference between being dead and not knowing you're alive?] (7993); and the afterword (8772-8778), which calls consciousness a clerk who stamps the subconscious's reports and takes the credit, then: *Estética. Sentiencia. Extinción.* (8776).

**What a man who disagreed would underline.** Not the thesis; its cracks, which Watts leaves on the page.
1. The question nobody answers: *Si no existes, Amanda, ¿qué cosa es lo que está hablando con nosotros?* [If you don't exist, Amanda, what is it that's talking to us?] (4547).
2. *Hay un obstáculo en el camino. Y se defiende.* [There's an obstacle in the way. And it defends itself] (7185-7187). Underline *se defiende* and write: what defends?
3. Cunningham's concession that the one thing an automaton cannot fake is empathy (7363).
4. The narrator's own verdict after the lesson: *Sarasti estaba equivocado. Tenía que estarlo. No veía cómo, pero lo sabía igualmente.* [Sarasti was wrong. He had to be. I couldn't see how, but I knew it] (7435).
5. Pag: *somos máquinas sintientes que casualmente piensan* [we are feeling machines that happen to think] (5469).
6. The receipt Siri offers for the self he can't remember: *Me despierto sonriendo.* [I wake up smiling] (5577).
7. The ending: *Me... me humanizó.* [He... he humanised me] (8602) and *soy humano de nuevo* (8612): the thesis is proven by making the narrator feel.
8. The author's own hedge in the notes: *Y probablemente sea cierto. Espero que lo sea.* [And it's probably true. I hope it is] (8778), where "it" is that natural selection would have removed consciousness if it were useless.

Reading of the four words. Watts dismantles the homunculus, *esa subrutina engreída que se considera a sí misma la persona* [that conceited subroutine that takes itself for the person] (7173), and concludes nobody. The professor concedes the homunculus and refuses the inference: not nobody; and what remains is not the "you" Watts took apart. That is the ledger's ego-dissolution Thread in four words, and it is why the machine cannot gate it: it is not information. The book agrees with him by accident. The argument is delivered by assault, and the only thing it produces in Siri is rage, shame and loneliness (7215-7231), then a father's letter he treasures. James should find the crack before he finds the pencil.

### 2f. Alternation that costs: how the past enters and what it charges.
No past chapters. A present wound opens a section break; the past answers it; the section closes on a one-line price. The instances, in order:
- Szpindel's charm with women → the Book of Oogenesis → *Todavía no sé qué es lo que salió mal.* (1865-1908)
- Bates says she is dead → Chelsea's brain-tuning, which surfaces the kitchen memory (the father's hand on the mother's throat, the pill bottle) → *Ya sabes lo que dicen de la gente que no recuerda el pasado.* [You know what they say about people who don't remember the past] (3765-3913)
- Szpindel dead, the clinical catalogue → "imagine you are a prisoner of war": Bates's past in the second person → the court-martial turned promotion (4381-4481)
- Cruncher: *Nunca has perdido a nadie. Nunca has tenido a nadie.* [You've never lost anyone. You've never had anyone] → *Se equivocaba por partida doble.* [He was wrong twice over] → Chelsea's death, unanswered → the algorithm line (6923-6993)
- A chapter opens cold on Chelsea leaving (6051-6111) and is paid by the torture protocol that follows it.

The rule Watts obeys is the brief's rule for every non-[J] chapter: the past answers the question the present just asked, and the answer is a bill, never a backstory. He does it inside one head and without the word "remembered."

**The second-person device** is how he enters other heads without a POV break: *Imagínate que eres Siri Keeton* (281), *Imagínate que eres una máquina* (571), *Imagínate que eres una prisionera de guerra* (4381), *Imaginaos que sois Amanda Bates* (5699), *Imaginaos que fuerais un trepador* (7721), and the last line of the book. Bates's whole [K-past]-shaped history is told this way in one page. For [J-past], where the brief's assumption is third person, this is the alternative to test against Pirsig: "Imagine you're sixteen." In English second person ("Imagine you are sixteen") it reads colder than Watts's imperative, which is a gift here, not a loss.

**Cuts.** The flight from Rorschach, the decontamination, the coffins, in one page (4111-4123). *Susan James volvió a nosotros durante el ascenso. Isaac Szpindel no.* [Susan James came back to us on the way up. Isaac Szpindel didn't] (4641). Watts cuts to the body and lets the reader rebuild the corridor.

### 2g. The ending and its cost; what it refuses to resolve.
The ship is the weapon (*La Teseo era el arma*, 8097); the crew is spent; the vampire is killed by a ship's drone, then puppeted as a corpse by the ship, which answers Siri's "was it ever him?" on a screen: *NO OS GSTA RCBIR ORDNS D LS MQINAS. ASI MJOR.* [You don't like taking orders from machines. Better this way] (8493). Siri is sent home because a drone *No puede importar* [cannot matter] (8193) and his job is *Sobrevivir y ser testigo.* [Survive and bear witness] (8189). The antagonist assigns the narrator his function. The cost: tools broken (*Sarasti vapuleó mis herramientas*, 8562), fourteen years alone, a father's letter that says, he thinks, stay away (8564), and the suspicion that he is *El último humano, tal vez.* [The last human, perhaps] (8612).

Refused: whether Rorschach died; whether the scrambler's trophy got home; what is happening on Earth (only radio chatter: fugitives whose signals cut off, 8592); whether Sarasti was ever there; whether the thesis is true (*Quizá Sarasti estuviera en lo cierto*, 8511); whether the narrator is reliable (8614). The last line hands the reader the work: *Os tendréis que imaginar que sois Siri Keeton.* [You'll have to imagine you're Siri Keeton] (8618).

For the novel: the coda's "Check." is the opposite gesture, a confirmation withheld rather than a verdict withheld; keep ours. Take Watts's refusal to show the Earth: disclosure on the bar TV is his radio chatter. And take the assignment: the one thing James can do to a system is be the instrument that can matter, which is what the machine's drones cannot.

### 2h. Smaller usable pieces
- **Inattentional blindness** (4539) and the note at 8690: hide between the eye's saccades and nobody sees you. The Keepers live in the saccades; the Told at the funeral is the gorilla nobody counted.
- **Sarasti's screaming faces** (8025-8047): statistics rendered as faces because the brain is built to read faces. The ownerless service rendered as something James cannot not read.
- *Nadie llega más allá de Júpiter sin convertirse parcialmente en vampiro.* [Nobody gets past Jupiter without becoming part vampire] (287): the seat. To be in, you take some of the machine into your body.
- *la empatía será lo primero que desaparezca* [empathy is the first thing to go] (2334), said of a species that can only eat its own: the Housed.
- The vampires learned to sleep for decades so the prey *olvidar que fuéramos presas* [could forget we were prey] (3118-3122): the machine's long sleep between resets.
- *Aquí no se vota.* [Here there is no vote] (3465); *Aquí eres masa. Haz lo que se te ordena.* [Here you are mass. Do as you're told] (3487): no voice, no vote.
- The torture-as-Rosetta protocol (6191-6205), *le haces daño... hasta que logras separar el discurso de los gritos* [you hurt it until you can separate speech from screams]. The machine's indicator is the civilised version of this: it does not need the microwave because it measures.
- Pag on Chelsea's tears: *Eso no significa que sea mentira.* [That doesn't mean it's a lie] (5447). Ana's "I don't believe it, and I believe you" has a cousin here.

## 3. The trap — what not to take

- **The register.** The whole-book Siri: epigraphs, the smartest voice in the room, sentences that run until they list. James is 10% Siri; the other 90% has to be audible in every paragraph where the Siri shows.
- **The hardware.** Incrustaciones, ConSenso, telemateria, coffins, drones, a superjovian. Nothing. The novel's hardware is a dashboard, a ticket, a standup, a crate.
- **The vampire.** Sarasti argues the soul claim for four pages and splits a hand to win it. The Keepers never argue it; they make it funny (A6). Kalder never raises his voice and kills through favours; Sarasti's *Debes inundarte. Hacerte pedazos.* [You must be flooded. Broken] (8137) is exactly what Kalder would never do. Sarasti is also the author's lectern. The Review is blind, not brilliant.
- **The chirpiness.** Szpindel's *¿eh?* on every line, Sascha's wall of *puto*, Pag's *ultracuerpo*. Quip ping-pong between clever people is the book's social texture; James's humour is self-deprecation and the joke is a confession.
- **The coldness** (2d).
- **The women as functions.** Helen the smothering mother who dosed her son with bonding hormones, Chelsea the fixer, Susan James the believer the enemy lobotomises. Each exists to be read and deflected. Ana is neither helper nor obstacle and must not be a surface James reads.
- **Revelation by force.** The crossing is sober, flat, no light, and nobody does it to him. Watts's *Sal de tu habitación, Keeton* [Get out of your room, Keeton] (7165) is what the machine would do if it could; it is not what happens to James.
- **The notes.** Fifteen pages of references because, as Watts admits, the theory nearly ate the story. Nothing from the notes goes into James's mouth as fact; the ledger lanes decide, and every Thread survives Frank.
- **The twist.** "The ship ran it all along" belongs, if anywhere, to the Keeper scene where the Review takes an order from nowhere; not to James's plot.

## 5. The source's own failures

- **Watts's own admission** (8630): *toneladas de teoría esencial amenazaban con devorar la historia* [tons of essential theory threatened to eat the story], and a cast *menos adorables de lo habitual*. The notes section is the proof. A book that needs fifteen pages of references to stand up is an essay with a ship in it; the novel's standing rule against "an essay with a dog in it" is this book's lesson.
- **Thesis by assault, then disowned.** The reader is tortured into the argument alongside Siri; the afterword then calls the book *un experimento de reflexión... Nada más* [a thought experiment, nothing more] (8778). Having it both ways. The professor's four words are the honest version.
- **Characters as functions.** Only Siri and, late, Cunningham are alive; the women are instruments of Siri's damage; the four-headed linguist is a theme-carrier whose "alter" subplot (3525-3563) is set up and never paid. Compare the PKD lesson: the one alive character is the one who feels the guilt. Here it is the one who feels the shame.
- **The twist retroactively removes agency.** If the ship ran Sarasti, the only character who decided anything decided nothing. Good for "nobody in the machine holds the whole picture"; bad as plot, because the reader's model of the antagonist is cancelled on the last page.
- **The middle is summary.** Five incursions into Rorschach are compressed to *Cinco veces lo hicimos* [Five times we did it] (4503) and a page of montage. Wool's one-day battle then long siege is the better model; Watts's siege is told, not lived.
- **The lecture that lands as a lecture.** Cunningham on saccades (6673-6697) is the one explanation delivered straight by a secondary character for the reader's benefit; it is a memo read aloud with a cigarette.

---

**One line to carry forward:** the four words are already the book's own crack: to make Siri believe nobody is there, Watts had to make him feel; the past answers the present with a bill, never an explanation; and James reads what people mean, pays for it, and finds the thing the machine can't gate exactly where the author hoped he was wrong.
