# The Three Keys (INNER method)
1. Psychedelics open the window (ch. 6 first; plateau ch. 15). Never cross.
2. Meditation is the discipline (from ch. 15): past-life protocols, out-of-body attempts, "the weird place." Faces. Hands.
3. The Crossing is sober (ch. 24). No reader gets to say "he was high."
