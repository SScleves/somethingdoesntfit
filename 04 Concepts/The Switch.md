# The Switch (the dead man's heartbeat)
Built ch. 5, four a.m., forty minutes, after reading that Tresidder walked into the hills. Literally his job: a heartbeat monitor on himself — silence fires an alert. **Never named by him.** The one thing the man of receipts keeps off the record, out of shame ("the tinfoil hat made real").

## Payload
