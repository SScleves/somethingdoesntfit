The story, chapter by chapter

Every chapter from THE BOOK in plain sentences, with its beats. Open a chapter to write what it should feel like, what it says, where it sits, what it adds and what to fix. A dot on the right shows where you've written, colored by the verdict you gave it.

Concepts. Open any chapter, part, journey stage or map node and press + Add a concept to attach one of the cases, theories and conspiracies in the library, then write how it would work there. The Journey and the Mind map can be edited too.

The hero's journey

The twelve stages of the journey, laid over this book. The line across the wheel is the threshold: above it is James's ordinary world, below it is the world he can't un-see. Tap a stage to read how the book does it, which chapters carry it, and to leave a note on whether it lands. Each stage has Edit chapters and Edit text, and you can set a chapter's stage from its card in Story.

Structure and pacing

The whole book as one strip, read left to right. Each block is a chapter, its width is its planned length, and its color is whose chapter it is. Underneath, the arcs of the main lines show where each one turns. Tap anything to see what it is. Santi's four chapters have no planned length yet, so they're drawn at a nominal width with a hatch.

    Mind map

    Everything the story is made of, around one center: the three truths, the machine, the people, the objects the reveals arrive as, the places, the real stories, the final reveals and the themes. Tap a node for the plain explanation, the chapters it lives in, and your notes. Drag to move, use the buttons or the wheel to zoom.

    Where every reveal goes

    The order the reader learns things in. The opening hooks with a conspiracy and a machine; the old stuff and the method fill the middle; the outrageous things and the truth come at the end, from the villain's own mouth. Each line links to the chapter where it lands.

    Recall

    Cards to keep the book in your head while you write: the machine, the people, the reveals, the rules, and the order of the chapters. Cards you get right come back less often. Your progress stays in this browser.

    Concept board

    A pinboard for ideas, questions, images and problems. Drag cards anywhere, tap one to edit it, and tie red string between cards that belong together. It starts with the book's core concepts pinned up; change, move or remove anything.

    All your notes

    Everything you've written, in book order: chapters first, then the journey, the map and the decisions. Use Copy all to paste it anywhere, or just tell Claude to read the notes; they're saved with the page.

    Verdicts, notes, concepts, journey and map edits as one text. Paste it to Claude and say "fold this into THE BOOK".
