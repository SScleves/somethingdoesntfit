    Mind map

    Everything the story is made of, around one center: the three truths, the machine, the people, the objects the reveals arrive as, the places, the real stories, the final reveals and the themes. Tap a node for the plain explanation, the chapters it lives in, and your notes. Drag to move, use the buttons or the wheel to zoom.

    Where every reveal goes

    The order the reader learns things in. The opening hooks with a conspiracy and a machine; the old stuff and the method fill the middle; the outrageous things and the truth come at the end, from the villain's own mouth. Each line links to the chapter where it lands.

    Recall

    Cards to keep the book in your head while you write: the machine, the people, the reveals, the rules, and the order of the chapters. Cards you get right come back less often. Your progress stays in this browser.

    Concept board

    A pinboard for ideas, questions, images and problems. Drag cards anywhere, tap one to edit it, and tie red string between cards that belong together. It starts with the book's core concepts pinned up; change, move or remove anything.

    All your notes

    Everything you've written, in book order: chapters first, then the journey, the map and the decisions. Use Copy all to paste it anywhere, or just tell Claude to read the notes; they're saved with the page.
