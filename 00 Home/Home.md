# Something Doesn't Fit — Novel Vault (rebuilt 8 Sep 2026)

**Premise:** A grieving expat who watches systems for a living finds one edited sentence in a dead professor's book — and follows a list of such edits into a lineage that has spent a century deleting the *giver* from the record, times civilizational resets, and is about to throw the switch again because the world is getting comfortable enough to ask questions.

**Thesis line (once, last page):** *The revolution is that he finally has time to love a dog.*
