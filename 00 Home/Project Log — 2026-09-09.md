## 0. What this project is

A **novel** adapting the thesis of the non-fiction essay *Something Doesn't Fit* (Santi's separate manuscript): human civilization runs in cycles of knowledge, collapse and suppression; the essay raises questions through anomalies and deliberately never answers them. **The novel's contract with the essay:** it does not answer *what happened* (the "how" of transcendence stays withheld). It answers the essay's *second* pattern — *how evidence is handled* — by catching the maintenance crew. The novel is where the plot is discovered and unmasked; the essay is the raw material, entering only as texture, never as lecture.

Working title: *Something Doesn't Fit* (the title is a reveal: the material conspiracy and the metaphysical truth are one seam). GitHub: `SScleves/somethingdoesntfit` (public as of 9 Sep 2026; readable via `git clone`).

## 1. Process decisions

- **Method:** hybrid — beat-sheet the skeleton (reveals, clock, payoffs), discovery-write inside beats. Pure King-style discovery was rejected because the book has late load-bearing reveals; pure outlining was rejected because it kills the grief voice.
- **Workflow:** I draft → Santi bleeds (marks lines he'd never say / close-but-off / his) → I re-register → lock. Santi assembles definitive versions himself and pastes into the vault; I supply paste-ready blocks or full chapter files. Since 9 Sep the repo is the exchange medium.
- **Locked chapters reopen only for continuity/canon, never for style.** Style discoveries go on the Revision Pass Checklist and are applied once, globally, after the full draft. (This rule was set when the question of retro-adding dialogue to ch. 1–2 came up: no.)
- **Obsidian vault** is the source of truth (built, then fully rebuilt 8 Sep as v2 after canon shifted). Folder scheme: 00 Home · 01 Manuscript · 02 Structure · 03 Characters · 04 Concepts · 05 Places · 06 Threads · 07 Voice · 08 Research · 09 The List. Canvas mind map in 00 Home.
- **Chapter-locked-beats check:** before handing over any full-chapter rebuild, I check it against that chapter's list of locked beats (instituted after I dropped the Ana scene from ch. 4 for three versions).
- **Advisor stance requested:** honest, in pursuit of the better book, not to please; hold positions unless real new information; lead with the uncomfortable thing.

## 2. Voice — how it was calibrated and what's locked

Chapter 1 was the calibration instrument. Key corrections from Santi that defined the voice:
- **No jargon.** "I work in IT." He thinks in *his* terms, also at work — never the reverse. Professional vocabulary is permitted only when he's actually working (on systems, or on the mystery). "Wall of green" became "wall of comfort" — the metaphor comes from him, not a dashboard.
- **The hobby is knowledge-hunting, not book-collecting** — strange websites, forums, people who talk too long; old books are one branch. Later refined: he hunts **discrepancies in claims across sources** ("retractions nobody admitted to"), not typos. A man who buys duplicates to compare is a trainspotter; this man is an investigator.
- **No Latin-flavor details** (no *mija*, no *Abuela*, no Chía) — I had projected ethnicity from the Spanish-edition memory; Santi called it out; it stays out unless he writes it.
- **Register recipe:** serious observation → concrete detail → joke that's actually a confession. Jokes deliver the seriousness; they never interrupt it ("More wine?").
- **Darkness budget:** one dark joke per few pages (the manifesto line, Blade). Dark = an implication he declines to examine, never a label.
- **Subtext stays subtext:** never state his wiring; "good at the demo" is the ceiling until Beat 12/ch. 25.
- **Swearing = punctuation** ("Fuck. I don't hate my job at all. But fuck."). Sparing.
- **Models:** Weir's narrator (competent, funny problem-solver; Rocky = the dogs) inside King's pacing (short scenes, chapter-end hooks, ordinary life warm before it breaks), aimed at Hartley's subject (*Sekret Machines* as market comp only). Watts's *Blindsight* studied for non-science texture (fragments as cuts, negative space, one aphorism per scene rationed) — never for coldness. Target ~30% texture. Holly-Gibney hedging ("I want to be accurate," "I don't want to oversell it").
- **Dialogue over monologue** when a fact can enter through conversation; Ana and the dogs are the licensed listeners; dialogue formatted as dialogue (Santi flagged buried dialogue).
- **Not Langdon** (Santi: "you're making the guesses too fast"): insights *happen to him* — the dogs answer a question he wasn't asking; every inference smaller than the last draft's; "I'm not the guy who finds a word and knows the secret of the pyramids by breakfast."
- **Killer-line problem (external critic, 9 Sep, confirmed):** too many landed aphorisms in succession make the reader experience the author, not the narrator. Nearly all offending lines were mine. Rule: one landed line per scene, max two per chapter; add deliberately flat connective sentences ("I checked the page again. Nothing. I went to bed."); no double landings; the narrator is not allowed to notice how good his sentences are. Specific cuts scheduled: "The Record is that personality with a filing system," "Reading is a man in the audience" (→ "Reading is safe."), "Not everything opens at four in the morning," flatten "The original said one thing…" neighbors, thin the "I want that on the record" refrain (it's also the switch's tell and must stay rare).
- **One beginning:** the search begins once (ch. 1, the drive home) and is never re-declared; later milestones are named as what they are.
- **"Language you almost know"** = INNER-track vocabulary only (Santi caught the reuse).

## 3. Characters — canon

Reads upside down; "you've got the face"; treaty: he washes, she dries; "submarine thing — you're right here and eight hundred meters down"; her argument is never *is it true* but *where are you*. - **H. M. Tresidder** (placeholder): reader in comparative mythology at a small English university (department gone). Author of *Waters Before Memory*, 1926. Vanished 1929, last seen leaving Urfa on foot heading east. Second impression **1931, printed in Leiden** for continental distribution. The Vanished. Knew what he'd written; the changer had *context*, not sharper reading. Plato's *Timaeus* (priest to Solon) is the idea's public ancestor: "nobody edits Plato; Plato is filed under story. Grandson of Tresidder's student (the student pursued it and was ridiculed/recanted). Annotated like he was arguing with authors (*no* — just *no*). His shelf = his conclusions (Ubik, A Canticle for Leibowitz, Blindsight still being bought at the end, Borges misfiled among sci-fi — Tlön, deliberate). He is the essay author who died before writing the argument; what he left is the List. Death: quiet, fast, plausible (ch. 14); a daughter: "he'd stopped sleeping."
- **The Keepers** (never named in-book): see §6.
- **Beard Man:** market dealer; "for all I know he's a Kevin"; mid-sentence since before the narrator was born; "twelve sounds better" (ambiguous — whimsy or completion; narrator resents every minute spent on it).
- **Frank:** young colleague, skull-emoji AI link; witness to the first slip ("Based on what, what?"); ch. 18 his tools arrive; the first convert in Act 3 — converts by reading a config diff.

## 6. The antagonists — canon

A lineage that survives resets with knowledge intact and *times* them: suffering is the leash, comfort is the crack; when questioning spreads downward they throw the kill switch. **Two tiers:** those who know (read the archive) and those who inherited (guard it, sincere, never read it). **Method: correct → ridicule → buy → vanish**; killing is the failure mode (a body is a fingerprint). Signature: the 1931 plate. They edit *instructions*, not facts — "they never fight stories, only instruments." Reader-aware: they hide in the blind spot of how tired people check. **Competence Ledger** rules: no unearned competence for him, no villain decay for them (never dumber than the 1931 plate; no monologues, no faces, no convenient clues); his edge is a *shape*, not a power — their invisibility is tuned to how people check, and he checks where they don't; they can't defend against him without abandoning the method, and the method matters more than any reader. Present fingerprints: foundations funding "heritage stabilization"; continuity bunkers; the AI defaults. Leverage on him: Ana, as pressure (the ch. 20 buy: "we don't disappear people; we hire them"). Institutional shape — open (ch. 20) — lean: a *custodianship* (a role, not a family).
